Record Packing

×
Batch Batch No. & Mfg date change every 3 months · Expiry = Mfg + 5 years
These bottles are added to the product's current batch. When the batch is 3 months old, the next packing starts a new batch automatically. Print the Batch No., Mfg and Expiry shown above on the label.
Automatically deducts 30 ml raw material + every applicable packaging item per unit, and adds to Finished Goods. Entries above the available stock are rejected.

✏️ Edit Finished Goods Row

×
Packed comes from "Record Packing"; Out / Damage / Repackaging come from Supervisor entries — all calculated by the system. Current Stock = First-Time Opening + Packed − Out + Repackaging. First-Time Opening is the stock on the day the system was started — entered once; daily opening/closing is calculated automatically.
